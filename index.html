▸ Map layers

Isometric / top-down map with a time scrubber. Buildings placed, order dots, order heat, kaaro's camera heat and trail, gather points.

 ▸ Time flows

Age lanes, production per minute, cumulative queues, build rate, APM and army-order pressure, with candidate fight moments.

What this can and can't show

A recorded game stores player commands, not game state. Everything here is rebuilt from those commands plus the starting header.

▸ Real: build placements, unit orders (target tiles + units selected), production queues, techs, age-ups (from chat), resign, the recorder's camera.

▸ Not available: resources, kills, unit positions, current population, the opponent's camera. "Pressure" is orders aimed at the enemy base, a proxy for fights rather than proof of one.

▸ Time is the replay's own sync clock (the in-game clock). Camera data is kaaro's only (the recorder).
